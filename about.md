# About
M13 "Monolit" (Mono1337) is a wireless 34-key split keyboard with a unibody design and built-in TrackPoint support.

## Inspiration
Like any other work, mine is based on the successful solutions of other authors and, in essence, represents their compilation, in one form or another, with my own "sense of beauty."

My respects.

[Jian](https://github.com/KGOH/Jian-Info) - Stagger \
[mEnki](https://github.com/aroum/mEnki) - Shape \
[Kabarga](https://github.com/aroum/kabarga) - LEDs \
[Moketa](https://github.com/inpudiy/MOKETA/) - Sockets mounting style \
Chieftain 42 - Design

## [M]ono(~~SP~~)[13]37
The name Monolit/Mono1337 transliteration of the Russian word "Монолит". \
M13 is short for Mono1337 and a reference to the Model M13.
